ADVANTAGES

    1. FARM TO FRONT DOOR

    Our quick A2 Desi Cow Milk home delivery from our farm to your doorstep happens within 24 hours, ensuring fresh milk, every morning!


    2. TECHNOLOGY TO THE RESCUE

    The entire milking process is untouched by human hands from grass to glass. Our automated milking parlour uses the latest technologies for efficient and safe milking.


    3. GOODBYE CHEMICALS

    Our milk is free from preservatives and additives. Similarly, we don't give our cows any growth hormones, ensuring pure milk, without any chemicals.

    4. A RELAXING RETREAT

    We prioritize happiness and the health of our cows, making them feel like they are on a retreat or a spa-vacation where they are nurtured, pampered and cared for.


    5. PURE DESI COWS

    We welcome breeds of Desi Cows like Sahiwal and Gir, but not any foreign cows at our farms. This ensures pure A2 milk, without any adulteration of A1.


    6. A PASSION FOR PERFECTION

    Backed by our passion to deliver perfection to our customers, we are able to excel at every step of the dairy production – whether it’s milking, quality control, packaging, online milk delivery in Pune, or customer service.
